WHY eps_plateau AND NOT amplification IS THE PRIMARY QUANTITY
  amplification = eps_plateau/eps_step is unstable wherever eps_step
  passes through zero. In the first run the grid maximum was 1271x at
  a point whose eps_step (3.5e-5) was the grid MINIMUM while its
  eps_plateau (0.044) was BELOW the median. That ratio measures a
  vanishing denominator, not a large error. Amplification is still
  reported, but as a distribution, never as a headline maximum.
  Note also log(amp) = log(eps_plateau) - log(eps_step) identically,
  so corr(log eps_step, log amp) is partly tautological.
