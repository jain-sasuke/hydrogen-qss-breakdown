\chapter{State Ordering}
\label{app:states}

Every index that appears in this thesis -- $\Lmat_{ij}$, a shell sum, a figure
axis labelled by state, the benchmark $\Lmat[23,5]$ -- refers to a position in
one fixed 43-element ordering. This appendix records that ordering so any index
in the text can be resolved without reading the code, states the one assumption
the ordering embeds, and names the file that produces it.

\section{What the state space is, and where it is cut}

The model tracks 43 states of neutral hydrogen: the 36 orbital-angular-momentum
resolved levels $n\ell$ for $n \le 8$, and seven $\ell$-bundled shells for
$9 \le n \le 15$. The proton is not a forty-fourth state; it is held as an
external reservoir of density $\nion$, which is the open-system boundary
condition discussed in Section~\ref{sec:two_references}.

The cut at $n=8$ is placed where it is because the observable is built from
$n=3$ and $n=4$. Both are fully $\ell$-resolved and both sit well below the
resolved/bundled boundary: $n=4$ five shells below it and eleven below the
truncation at $n_{\max}=15$, $n=3$ six and twelve.
Resolving $\ell$ costs states quadratically -- $n^2$ sublevels per shell -- and
buys nothing above the shells the diagnostic reads, provided the bundled shells
are $\ell$-statistical, which Section~\ref{sec:bundling_check} tests rather than
assumes.

Degeneracies are hydrogenic throughout: $g_{n\ell} = 2(2\ell+1)$ for a resolved
sublevel and $g_n = 2n^2$ for a shell, the factor $2$ being electron spin.
Summing the resolved sublevels of each shell returns $2n^2$ exactly for every
$n$ from 1 to 8, which is as far as the resolved levels go; each bundled state
carries $2n^2$ by construction. The degeneracies of all 43 states sum to
$\sum_{n=1}^{15} 2n^2 = 2480$. Ionisation potentials are hydrogenic,
$I_p = \chi_{\mathrm{H}}/p^2$ with $\chi_{\mathrm{H}} = \SI{13.6058}{\electronvolt}$;
the product $I_p\,p^2$ runs from $13.6057995$ to $13.6058006$ across the table,
constant to seven significant figures.

\section{The assumption the ordering embeds}

Bundling shells $9 \le n \le 15$ into single states asserts that the population
inside each of those shells is distributed statistically over $\ell$,
$n_{n\ell} = (g_{n\ell}/g_n)\,n_n$. Nothing in the ordering enforces this; it is
a modelling choice made when the rate coefficients were summed, and it is the
reason no $\ell$-mixing rate appears among states 36--42. Whether it is
justified is a physical question, tested in Section~\ref{sec:bundling_check} by
comparing the intra-shell $\ell$-mixing rate against the processes that remove
population from the shell.

This assumption has a consequence that matters for Chapter~\ref{ch:validation}:
the bundled sub-block of $\Lmat$ is, by construction, the statistical-$\ell$
limit. A bundled operator cannot represent a non-statistical $\ell$
distribution, so any comparison against a published tabulation that is itself
bundled in $\ell$ is comparing two models that agree on this point by
construction rather than by calculation.

\section{Two hydrogen ionisation energies}

Two slightly different values of the hydrogen ionisation energy appear in the
rate pipeline, and the difference is physical rather than an inconsistency:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\SI{13.605693122990}{\electronvolt}$, the Rydberg energy $R_\infty hc$,
    is the threshold used by the collision datasets (CCC, TICS) and therefore
    sets every collisional threshold energy.
  \item $\SI{13.598434599702}{\electronvolt}$, the measured ionisation energy of
    the hydrogen atom including reduced-mass and relativistic corrections, sets
    the radiative transition energies.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Both are recorded in \texttt{L\_meta.csv} as
\texttt{IH\_collision\_eV} and \texttt{IH\_radiative\_eV}
(\texttt{assemble\_cr\_matrix.py}, lines 76--77). They differ by
\SI{0.053}{\percent}. The hazard this creates is that a detailed-balance
relation evaluated with one energy on one side and the other on the other side
will fail at the few-tenths-of-a-percent level for reasons that look like a data
error and are not. Both balance relations in this model use a single ladder
throughout and close far below that level.

\section{Provenance}

The ordering is generated by \texttt{src/rates/assemble\_K\_exc.py} (line 361)
and written to
\texttt{data/processed/collisions/K\_exc\_full/state\_index.csv}. It is read by
\texttt{src/validation/cr\_context.py}, which every verification script in this
thesis imports rather than redeclaring the ordering locally; the number of
states is taken as \texttt{L\_grid.shape[-1]} and the ground index as
$\arg\min_p n_p$, so neither is hard-coded anywhere in the analysis.

As a structural check on the assembled operator, the columns of $\Lmat$ sum to
$-\nel K_{\mathrm{ion}}(p)$ -- ionisation is the only process that removes
population from the 43-state manifold -- and this holds to a maximum relative
deviation of $1.8\times10^{-12}$ across the 43 states at
$\Te = \SI{10}{\electronvolt}$, $\nel = \SI{1e12}{\per\cubic\centi\metre}$, and
$1.1\times10^{-12}$ at $\nel = \SI{1e15}{\per\cubic\centi\metre}$
(\texttt{verify\_fujimoto\_bundle.py}, gate 2).
