  P3 as written ('at every pair') is refuted by the 15 rows listed in STEP 4; the direction of the
  effect (rising source -> larger error) holds at every heating pair and at m = 5 everywhere.
  A fast-exchanging reservoir with a steady source freezes the closed-
  parcel error rather than removing it; a rising source enlarges it at
  the heating pairs and at large multipliers everywhere. 'Upper estimate'
  is therefore not what tau_esc < tau_slow implies. The chapter 5
  magnitudes are conditional on the reservoir history.

  Assumptions of this toy (all stated, none tested here): 0-D parcel;
  one escape time per pair, taken at the pre-step Te and held through
  the step; recycling source independent of u; no ionisation-front
  motion, no molecular channel, no spatial source profile; source
  multipliers illustrative. The toy fixes the sign of the inference,
  not the magnitude of the divertor error.
