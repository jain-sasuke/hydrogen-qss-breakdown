READING. The chapter's mechanism is refuted and its conclusion survives for a different reason.
  (1) The rates are not l-blind. Adjacent upward transfer out of (n, l) varies across l by a factor that GROWS with n (1.22 at
      n = 2, 2.5-2.8 at n = 9) and downward transfer by 5-6 at n = 7-10, largest at the yrast sublevel in every case (R1); in
      population-weighted terms the scatter at n = 9 and 1 eV is a coefficient of variation 0.27 upward and 0.46 downward
      (weighted mean |K/K_mean - 1| 0.23 and 0.39), so the edge-to-edge factors overstate the typical departure by about
      two, and 27-46 % scatter is still not 'close to l-blind'. The transfer into a shell from a statistically populated neighbour
      is not statistical either: from below, b runs from 0.28 (l' = 0) to 1.66 (yrast) at n_f = 9 and 10; from above, 0.54 to
      1.37 (R2). P1-P3 predicted the opposite trend and are not reproduced.
  (2) The statistical distribution is nevertheless the fixed point wherever the neighbour shells are in mutual Boltzmann ratio,
      because every l-resolved rate obeys detailed balance (G1): b_l - 1 = (theta - 1)[f_dn(l) - mean f_dn] with |f_dn - mean| <= 0.16,
      an identity (checked to 6e-04), so R3's b_l = 1 is not a test of the rates; its content is theta. At the 34 grid points the
      121 combinations occupy, theta_9 - 1 lies within 6e-05 (R3) and every bundled pair within 6e-05 (R3b), and theta_9 stays
      within 3e-4 of 1 when the bundled shells' radiative rates are set to zero or raised to A(np_total) (R5), so it is not an
      artefact of the bundled block's own assumptions. The stronger statement is the one the pipeline's own ionisation/three-body
      pair supports: the high shells are in Saha-Boltzmann equilibrium there, b_Saha(n) - 1 within 9e-05 for n = 8..15 at all 34
      points, which covers n = 11..15 (LTE sublevels are statistical whatever rates exist) and covers the ionisation channel that
