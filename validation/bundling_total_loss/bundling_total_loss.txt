What this does and does not say. The original test compared the mixing with the l-DIFFERENTIAL loss (radiative decay of np, the channel
that would de-populate one l preferentially); that margin is >= 1538. Against the TOTAL loss the margin shrinks to a few because the
dominant loss at these densities is collisional n-changing, which RESULT 5 shows goes overwhelmingly to the adjacent shells. Whether
an n-changing collision at high n disturbs the l-distribution is a physics question this script does not answer; it supplies both numbers.
