==============================================================================
WHAT REPLACES THE TWO SENTENCES IN SECTION 6.3
==============================================================================
(a) The factorisation and the tanh ceiling do NOT hold unchanged for a
    matrix with a third reservoir. The excited block is affine in each
    reservoir with non-negative coefficients (item 5); at fixed molecular
    strength the ground-fed fraction is a logistic in ln u and
    max_u|F3-F4| = tanh(|Delta(v)|/4) with Delta depending on v (item 4).
    That is a bound on the response to the ground reservoir alone. It says
    nothing about the response to the molecular reservoir, and Delta(v)
    changes sign as the molecular fraction grows.
(b) The dilution f_p -> f_p(1-phi_p) is exact for the LOCAL sensitivity at
    the reservoir value where phi_p is measured, but the finite-step error
    is exp(Sbar Lambda)-1, not linear, and the 0.201/0.054/0.053/0.019
    sensitivities correspond to no point of this model. Computed values
    for (0.6, 0.3) and the other targets are in the tables above.
